# RedChamber-ZHJA-CulturalParallel

A Chinese–Japanese parallel corpus of culturally loaded expressions from *Dream of the Red Chamber*.

This repository accompanies our paper:

> **On the Systematic Challenges of Culturally Loaded Machine Translation:  
> Dream of the Red Chamber as the Cultural Lens**  
> Findings of EMNLP 2026

---

## Overview

Culturally loaded expressions encode meanings that are deeply rooted in specific social, historical, and cultural contexts. Translating such expressions requires more than surface-level linguistic correspondence, as their meanings often depend on cultural knowledge shared by members of the source community.

To facilitate research on culturally loaded machine translation, we construct a Chinese–Japanese parallel corpus based on *Dream of the Red Chamber* (《红楼梦》), one of the most representative works of classical Chinese literature.

The corpus contains Chinese source segments with culturally loaded expressions and their corresponding Japanese reference translations.

The complete dataset described in our paper contains **500 aligned Chinese–Japanese segments**, covering five cultural categories.

This repository currently releases an **initial subset of 100 segments**. The remaining data will be added in future updates.

---

## Dataset

The current release contains:

- **100 Chinese–Japanese parallel segments**
- **Source language:** Chinese (`zh`)
- **Target language:** Japanese (`ja`)
- **Domain:** Classical Chinese literature / culturally loaded translation
- **Source text:** *Dream of the Red Chamber* (《红楼梦》)
- **Reference translation:** Japanese translation by **Ito Sohei (伊藤漱平)**

Each source segment contains culturally loaded linguistic or cultural information, while the corresponding Japanese text serves as the **human reference translation**.

The Japanese references are not translations generated by large language models.

---

## Cultural Categories

Following the cultural taxonomy adopted in our paper, the dataset is organized into five categories:

| Category | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `ecology` | Flora, fauna, climate, geographical landscapes, and other natural phenomena carrying cultural meaning |
| `religion` | Religious beliefs, folk beliefs, ancestor worship, superstition, and mythological concepts |
| `material` | Clothing, food, tools, artifacts, and other elements of material culture |
| `linguistics` | Idioms, proverbs, slang, riddles, fixed expressions, and culturally embedded rhetorical forms |
| `society` | Social structures, customs, institutions, kinship systems, etiquette, and interpersonal conventions |

The complete dataset contains 100 samples for each category.

The current 100-sample release contains a subset of these data.
